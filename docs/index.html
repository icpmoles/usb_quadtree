Quadtree visualization of USB Vendor IDS

How to read:

Assume to have your USB Vendor ID VID in hexadecimal: 0x0A6B.
You can split it in two parts: 0x0A and 0x6B
The Most Significant Bytes (MSB) = 0x0A will be the X axis, while the Least Significant Bytes (LSB) = 0x6B will be the Y axis.
Keep in mind that X goes to the left while Y points down. (Convention used in computer graphics and MatPlotLib)

Hover over a point

Observations

  • A lot of VIDs have low MSB, but at the same time they prefer not to go too low. (◧)
  • The LSB seem more uniformly distributed.
  • There is a subtle diagonal (⇱) with MSB ≈ LSB if you really squint.
  • There is also a subtle upper-left triangle (◤), which may indicate that MSB + LSB = Constant.
    (But maybe I'm just reading too much into it)
